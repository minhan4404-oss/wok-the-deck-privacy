游戏无需注册或登录。游戏进度、设置和语言偏好保存在你的设备上，供继续游玩使用。当前 Android 版本没有游戏内广告、第三方分析 SDK，也不通过游戏收集或向开发者服务器传送个人数据。游戏不会在应用内与其他公司分享个人数据。

购买和平台服务

游戏下载、付款、安装及平台层面的诊断由 Google Play 处理，受 Google 自身的隐私政策约束。游戏开发者不会在游戏内取得你的银行卡或付款资料。

保存与删除

设备上的游戏资料会保留，直到你在游戏中重置进度、清除应用数据或卸载游戏。游戏不设线上账号，因此开发者没有可供提取或删除的云端游戏存档。

Wok the Deck Privacy Policy

Android package: com.minhang.wokthedeck · Updated September 29, 2026

Data handled by the game

The game requires no account or sign-in. Progress, settings, and language preferences are stored on your device so you can continue playing. The current Android version contains no in-game ads or third-party analytics SDK and does not collect personal data through the game or send it to a developer server. The game does not share personal data with other companies from within the app.

Purchases and platform services

Google Play handles download, payment, installation, and platform-level diagnostics under Google's own privacy policy. The game developer does not receive your bank card or payment details inside the game.

Retention and deletion

On-device game data remains until you reset progress in the game, clear app data, or uninstall the game. There is no online account or cloud game save held by the developer.

Contact

If you choose to email the developer, we use your message and reply address to handle your request and respond. You may request deletion of the email correspondence at the same address: minhan4404@gmail.com.

Updates

If the game's data practices change, we will update this page and the related Google Play disclosures, with a new update date.
